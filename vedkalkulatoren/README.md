# Vedkalkulatoren

A calculator for Norwegian households: what heat from firewood really costs next to electricity

*Hva koster varmen fra ved, sammenlignet med strøm?*

Choose the wood species, how it is sold and your stove, and the calculator gives the real price per kilowatt-hour of heat from firewood. It sets that next to electricity at spot price, after the electricity subsidy and grid fees. The page lists the sources for wood density, moisture and stove efficiency, and what you type is not sent anywhere. On arrodi.com the site also counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics.

**For:** Norwegian households that heat with wood or are thinking about it

Use it on the site: https://arrodi.com/work/vedkalkulatoren
